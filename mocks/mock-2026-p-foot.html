About

Mer So is the independent design studio of Jen Murse, creating thoughtful products for everyday life, drawing on more than 20 years of experience designing across digital and physical mediums.

The work begins with small moments that deserve more attention, like a few quiet minutes of reflection at the end of the week or the pile of inspiration saved for later. The intention is to make the everyday worth noticing, whether on a screen, on paper, or in something you can hold.

Everything is made at a small scale, with care for the people who use it.
